Objective

Why mathematics matters in computing

This project connects mathematical thinking to real-world digital systems. Every program, network, and algorithm depends on mathematical structures for correctness, speed, and decision-making.

We use four important ideas from the Foundation of Mathematics: Combinatorics, Numerical Methods, Operations Research, and Boolean Algebra.

Core idea

Mathematics is not only a theory subject in computer science. It is the engine behind secure passwords, optimization of resources, numerical approximation, and digital logic.

What I learned

Conclusion

This project showed that mathematics is deeply connected to computing. Combinatorics helps calculate risk and possible password combinations. Numerical methods improve approximate solutions for equations that do not have direct formulas. Linear programming supports resource optimization and efficient distribution. Boolean algebra forms the logic behind digital gates, conditions, and search filters.

In real life, software engineers use these ideas every day to protect systems, design efficient algorithms, optimize server usage, and ensure reliable digital operations.
